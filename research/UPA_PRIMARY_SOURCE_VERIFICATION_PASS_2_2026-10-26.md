Date: 2026-10-26. **Status: substantive additional findings, not a completed exhaustive architectural survey.**

## 1. Previously overlooked room-type contradiction (high importance)

UPA's published **2023 Western Region accommodation rates** document lists THREE Yeoval room categories:
- Standard **shared room with ensuite: 26 m²**
- Standard **shared room without ensuite: 26 m²**
- Standard **single room with ensuite: 16 m²**

Source: https://www.upa.org.au/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Accommodation_Rates_WR.pdf

UPA's **current Yeoval public page** says single and shared rooms **all have an ensuite**. Source: https://www.upa.org.au/locations/dubbo/yeoval-aged-care/

**Do not flatten this discrepancy.** Possible explanations include renovation, discontinued category, outdated 2023 rates or marketing simplification. None is proven. For 3D reconstruction, do not assign an ensuite to every historic room or assume all shared rooms remain. The 16 m² and 26 m² figures are provider-advertised room areas, NOT surveyed wall-to-wall dimensions, and room category does not establish count, exact footprint or wing location. Evidence tier B; current room inventory unresolved.

## 4. Applicable design guidance — verified specific item

National Aged Care Design Principles and Guidelines, **Guideline 2.7 Private Bedrooms**, recommends:
- Private lockable bedrooms with external windows and resident control over furnishings.
- Bedrooms accessed from corridors rather than directly from communal rooms, with staggered entries to support privacy and wayfinding.
- Space to manoeuvre equipment around beds.
- Privacy between bed areas where shared rooms remain, and movement towards single occupancy.

**Important:** These are forward-looking design recommendations, not evidence that Yeoval's older existing bedrooms comply or that shared rooms do not exist. In fact, UPA's public Yeoval page currently advertises shared bedrooms. The game should reflect this difference between recommended new design and actual documented room offerings.

Source: https://www.health.gov.au/sites/default/files/2024-08/national-aged-care-design-principles-and-guidelines_0.pdf

## 5. Regulation and code — verified scope, no false certification

- **NCC 2022** defines Class 9c aged care buildings as residential accommodation for aged persons requiring personal care and 24-hour staff assistance to evacuate in an emergency. Classification of any particular Yeoval structure requires a competent authority's determination and review of approvals. Source: https://ncc.abcb.gov.au/system/files/ncc/ncc2022-consolidated-performance-requirements-20230501b.pdf
- **Strengthened Aged Care Quality Standards** applied from **1 November 2025**. Relevant outcomes: 4.1b safe/comfortable environments, 4.2 infection prevention and control, 6.4 dining experience, 7.1 daily living. These are provider duties, NOT proof of compliance at Yeoval. Sources: https://www.agedcarequality.gov.au/providers/quality-standards/strengthened-aged-care-quality-standards and https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2025L01173/2025-12-13/2025-12-13/text/1/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html
- **AS 1428, AS 3745, AS 1668 and AS 2896** applicability, precise clauses, editions, concessions, alteration triggers and on-site conformity remain **NOT VERIFIED**. No blanket code-compliance claim is justified.
