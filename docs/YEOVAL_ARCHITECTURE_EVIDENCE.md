# Yeoval Aged Care: detailed architectural evidence register

**Compiled:** 2026-10-26 (Australia/Sydney). **Address:** 3–7 Lord Street, Yeoval NSW 2868.  
**Site use:** aged care, co-located home care and community health.  
**Purpose:** reproducible evidential foundation for a 3D reconstruction, *not* an architectural as-built document.

| Total land area | **Unknown** | Unknown | Need parcel/DP areas across ALL site titles |
| Main footprint area | **Unknown** | Unknown | Needs georeferenced aerial delineation or DA drawings |
| Building width/length | **Unknown** | Unknown | No calibrated floor plan identified |
| Building height / roof slope | **Unknown** | Unknown | Photos alone do not give a calibrated measurement |

**Do not derive a made-up 5.2 × 5.0 m room from 26 m².** Many length/width combinations have that area; even shape and area inclusions are unknown.

## Architecture visible in public imagery

- [Caring Co / nursing home](https://www.caringco.com.au/providers/yeoval-community-nursing-home) publishes a frontal photo. Visually verified: red/orange brick wall, light-coloured gabled covered front entry, triangular lattice panel, UPA crest, white/cream entrance posts, glazed central double doors, paved approach, planting/landscaping. **Viewpoint and image date not verified**; use as photoreference only.
- [UPA official brochure, PDF p. 2](https://www.myagedcare.gov.au/sites/default/files/attachments/25/2-1X3TIX25.pdf) shows a communal room: wood-toned floor, dining table and chairs, curtained windows, ceiling fan, pale walls, ceiling fittings. **The photo is not a scale drawing**, and could be a promotional/general image; confirm it depicts Yeoval before fixing placements.
- Facility interior spaces advertised in [UPA brochure](https://www.myagedcare.gov.au/sites/default/files/attachments/25/2-1X3TIX25.pdf): single rooms with ensuites, large lounges and activity rooms, onsite catering kitchen, communal dining, library, outdoor BBQ, hair salon and landscaped outdoor areas.
- [UPA current listing](https://www.upa.org.au/locations/dubbo/yeoval-aged-care/) describes single/double shared ensuite bedrooms, dining room, home care, community health centre and dementia-specific care.
- [Caring Co](https://www.caringco.com.au/providers/yeoval-community-nursing-home) describes two large lounge areas, activities rooms and foyer. Do not assume exact count / sizes are verified by design drawings.

## DA documentation retrieval

**Cabonne Council** is the correct local planning authority: [Development Application Tracker landing page](https://www.cabonne.nsw.gov.au/Development/Development-Applications/Development-Application-Tracker); [JavaScript DA application register](https://cabonne.greenlightopm.com/search-register?deptName=Development). The web-visible page offers searches by application number, application type, address, and date; it does not itself expose downloadable drawings without interacting with its dynamic search.

Target requests, in priority order:
1. **2012 UPA commissioned site plan** cited in the annual report; request the drafter name, drawing register and original measured drawing.
2. **2015–2016 construction of a covered walkway** joining existing two wings; ask for approved site plans, floor plans, elevations, construction drawings, as-built survey and approvals.
3. **2018–2022 Netherwood western wing** proposal; ask for its design/DA number, approval status, layout and whether six beds actually reopened.
4. Full property **Lot/DP survey** and boundary dimensions for **1 / 3 / 5 / 7 Lord Street** and any amalgamated title.
5. **2026 current external massing survey** and non-sensitive public floor plan from UPA.
6. Historic satellite photos 2012, 2016, 2018, 2023, 2026 to identify changes (record year, projection, scale, uncertainty).

## Recommended modelling safeguards

For Repo Town/3D modelling, save *only* verified geometry in the 'surveyed' layer. Store inferred dimensions with method/uncertainty and label *approximation*, never a true plan. Render two wings joined by a covered walkway as a historically grounded **concept** until exact footprints are obtained. Exclude detailed resident room placements, staff-only/security-controlled routes or systems from any public reconstruction unless UPA authorises a public-safe plan. Use generic interiors for any undetermined clinical/service areas.
